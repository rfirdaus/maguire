FAQ

Questions?

Which tender portals does Maguire support?

Maguire is being designed to aggregate tender opportunities from multiple Indonesian procurement sources. Coverage will expand progressively during beta.

Can Maguire read tender PDF documents?

Maguire is designed to analyze tender documentation and extract important eligibility, administrative, technical, and commercial requirements.

Will Maguire automatically decide whether we should join a tender?

No. Maguire is designed as a decision-support system. It surfaces requirements, gaps, and risks while leaving the final decision to your team.

Can multiple employees collaborate on a tender?

Team collaboration is part of the product direction, including ownership, task tracking, and document preparation.

Is Maguire already available?

Maguire is currently in early development. Companies interested in testing the platform can request early access.
